ABOUT US



We are an Agra, India based company dealing in a variety of Products and Commodities with a manufacturing industry for Metals in SIDCUL Industrial Area Rd, Rudrapur,Uttarakhand and Vatva Industrial Area, Ahmedabad Gujarat in India. We are one of the leading exporters and are registered since 2020.

Our main export markets are Gulf, Africa, South East Asia, South America and Europe and we are open to new markets.
